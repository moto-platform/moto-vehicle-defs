**Modular Split-Mezzanine Perfboards (`Board A / B / C`), Standoffs, IP67 Enclosures, EMI Shielding & Tools:**

> **Design Rule — Modular Split-Mezzanine Perfboards over One Large Board:** Never cover `NUCLEO-H755ZI-Q` (`133.3×70 mm`) with a single monolithic `9×15 cm` perfboard (which flexes like a drumhead under vibration, couples 12 V switching noise into analog/safety circuits, blocks the `ST-LINK` USB/LEDs, and prevents unplugging Scale 1B external sensors). Instead, split the 2nd story inside the `170×120×55 mm` Main Box into **3 independent modular double-sided epoxy perfboards**:
> 1. **`Board A` (`5×7 cm` — Core Power & CAN, Scale 1A):** `1N5822`, `SMBJ30CA TVS`, `HW-788 5V Buck`, `INA226`, `1 kHz` battery divider, `SN65HVD230 ×2`, `microSD`. Can be bench-tested at 12–14 V (**Gate B1**) before plugging into `NUCLEO-H755ZI-Q`.
> 2. **`Board B` (`7×9 cm` — Scale 1B External Anomaly Conditioners):** `MAX31856` EGT amp, `PC817` optocouplers, `3× 10K NTC` dividers, `74HC14` spark Schmitt trigger, `LJ12A3` level-shifter. Unplugging `Board B` cleanly converts the hardware to **Scale 1A (CAN-only)** with zero desoldering.
> 3. **`Board C` (`4×6 cm` — M4 Safety Partition & Rigid IMUs):** `MPU-6500` (M4), `MPU-6590` (M7), `74HC123` dead-man & `WS2812B` 5 V buffer. Mounted on **short `6 mm` rigid brass standoffs** on the enclosure floor / frame bracket (never on the tall 2nd-story columns).
